# Supply Chain Delivery Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes delivery performance across a global e-commerce supply chain to identify operational bottlenecks, understand the causes of delayed shipments, and evaluate their impact on business profitability.

Using Python and exploratory data analysis (EDA), the project uncovers patterns in shipping performance, regional operations, customer segments, and profitability to generate actionable business recommendations.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Measure overall delivery performance
- Identify factors contributing to shipment delays
- Analyze the relationship between delays and profitability
- Detect operational bottlenecks across the supply chain
- Provide data-driven recommendations for improving delivery efficiency

## Key Insights

- More than half of the analyzed orders were delivered late.
- Shipping mode was identified as a major contributor to delivery delays.
- Delayed deliveries created significant profitability risk.
- Delay patterns were observed across multiple operational dimensions.
- Seasonal demand spikes showed a measurable impact on delivery performance.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Review and optimize shipping mode allocation.
- Improve carrier performance monitoring.
- Strengthen payment processing workflows.
- Develop seasonal capacity planning strategies.
- Continuously monitor delivery KPIs to reduce operational inefficiencies.
